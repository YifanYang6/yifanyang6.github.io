  1. ISSTA
    Gleaner: A Semantically-Rich and Efficient Online Sampler for Microservice Diagnostics
    Yifan Yang, Aoyang Fang, Songhan Zhang, and 1 more author
    Apr 2026
    Accepted at the ACM SIGSOFT International Symposium on Software Testing and Analysis (ISSTA 2026)
    Abs PDF Code

    Distributed tracing in microservices is critical for diagnostics but generates overwhelming data volumes, making intelligent sampling necessary. Gleaner proposes an online tail-sampling framework for microservice diagnostics that avoids expensive graph analysis by representing traces as a semantically enriched bag-of-edges and using efficient set-based operations. It prioritizes anomalous and rare traces for downstream root cause analysis and reports strong gains in trace pattern coverage, diversity, and RCA accuracy.

  2. TOSEM
    Metis: An Interpretable and Unified Troubleshooting Framework for Microservices using Multi-modal Data
    Zhouruixing Zhu, Yifan Yang, Aoyang Fang, and 2 more authors

    Under review at ACM Transactions on Software Engineering and Methodology (TOSEM)
    Abs

    Metis studies troubleshooting for microservices with a unified and interpretable multi-modal framework. It combines diverse operational signals to support diagnosis and evaluation in complex microservice environments.

2025

  1. FSE
    Rethinking the Evaluation of Microservice RCA with a Fault Propagation-Aware Benchmark
    Aoyang Fang, Songhan Zhang, Yifan Yang, and 7 more authors
    Oct 2025
    Accepted at the ACM International Conference on the Foundations of Software Engineering (FSE 2026)
    Abs PDF Website

    This paper revisits how microservice root cause analysis methods are evaluated. It shows that widely used public benchmarks are often too simple, introduces a fault-propagation-aware benchmark generation framework, and reports a more challenging dataset for evaluating RCA methods under realistic operational complexity.

  2. ASE
    DynaCausal: Dynamic Causality-Aware Root Cause Analysis for Distributed Microservices
    Songhan Zhang, Aoyang Fang, Yifan Yang, and 3 more authors
    Oct 2025
    Under peer review; ASE 2026 submission
    Abs PDF

    DynaCausal proposes a dynamic causality-aware framework for root cause analysis in distributed microservices. It models time-varying dependencies across multi-modal operational signals and aims to improve causal attribution under dynamic service behavior.
